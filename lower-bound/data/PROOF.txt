PACK-BOUND: proof and verification notes

1. Pinned problem and conventions

The unchanged frozen price is out/prices.json, SHA-256
 aa3bad1ff635e6ac0ba916b58709cdb7688f164fa3c8b4507f8bf66f45b1a167.
Every JSON price is interpreted as its exact binary64 rational, not as an
arbitrarily rounded decimal. The target alpha is
 3502021035450561/9007199254740992.
Cells are (i,j), 0<=i,j<30, encoded as 30*i+j. Their orbit is the cyclic
distance min((i-j) mod 30,(j-i) mod 30). The marginal law is circulant and
even, with per-cell probability p_d. Logarithms are natural, h(t)=-t log t,
h(0)=0. For a block U, r_i and c_j are its row and column masses, m is its
total mass, and
 c(U)=2 sum_i h(r_i)+2 sum_j h(c_j)-h(m).
The residual is c(U)-sum_{z in U} gamma_z-phi(m).
The frozen windows are (lo_j/960,hi_j/960], j=0,...,480; they are contiguous,
cover (0,1], and have positive width. The proof checks both neighboring
closures, hence the larger penalty at an endpoint. All negative witnesses
used for the main obstruction lie strictly inside one window, so this
conservative convention and the original right-closed convention agree.

2. Obstruction to every valid node bound at the unchanged price

Let B={ (i,j): cyclic distance(i,j)<=11 }, and U=B minus {(0,4)}.
There are 30+2*11*30-1=689 cells. All 30 diagonal cells belong to U, so U is
in the d0_ALL root family without requiring a symmetry argument. Define
 s=p_0+2 sum_{d=1}^{11} p_d, a=p_4, and M=30*s-a.
All row masses except row 0 equal s; row 0 equals s-a. All column masses
except column 4 equal s; column 4 equals s-a. Therefore exactly
 c(U)=4*(29*h(s)+h(s-a))-h(M),
 gamma(U)=30*gamma_0+60 sum_{d=1}^{11} gamma_d-gamma_4.
For the unchanged price,
 gamma(U)=7943841111248305958655/590295810358705651712,
 phi_479=2424715621387805/18014398509481984.
Both the true-law and grid masses lie strictly in
 (319/320,959/960), the interior of window 479.
The grid mass is exactly 4497228775388919/4503599627370496.
The grid residual is rigorously in
 (-0.00232169076942,-0.00232169076940).
The true residual is approximately -0.002321690769306773; the full rational
outward enclosure is in out/rational_law_obstruction.json.

For any valid lower bound L on any node containing U,
 L <= inf_{V in node} residual(V) <= residual(U) < 0.
Every complete cover of d0_ALL has a leaf containing U. Consequently no
valid bound, LP/Lagrangian relaxation, dominance argument, branching rule,
or finite exhaustive enumeration can produce an all-positive cover of
this frozen point. This is an infeasible-price obstruction, stronger than
an obstruction to a particular relaxation. It says nothing about the
existence of another price proving C>2.

A second simple block B minus {(29,29)} belongs to d0_L29 and has true

-0.0000007961955755163435 (window 233). These latter two survive the

3. Independent true-law enclosure

Write a_j=sum_{r in Z} exp(-alpha*(j+30*r)^2),
 b_j=sum_k a_k*a_{j-k}, m2=b_0, m3=sum_j a_j*b_{-j},
 lambda_j=m2*a_j/b_j,
 rho_x=sum_j a_j*cos(2*pi*j*x/30),
 K_d=sum_j lambda_j*cos(2*pi*j*d/30).
The uniform average of all 30 translates gives
 p_d=K_d*sum_x rho_x^2*rho_{x+d}^2/(30^3*m2*m3).
This expression can also be obtained by averaging the component-law cells
q_0(x,x+d) directly. The fresh mpmath implementation uses the latter
average. The integer-only implementation uses the displayed expression.
The fresh mpmath probability boxes lie inside the inherited law boxes; the independent integer boxes contain all fresh mpmath probability boxes.

For 0<=d<=15, summing the Gaussian terms r=-2,...,2 leaves two positive
tails starting at magnitude L=90-d. Successive terms are bounded by a
geometric ratio exp(-alpha*(2*L*30+30^2))<exp(-1)<1/2, so both tails are
at most 4 exp(-alpha*L^2). This also encloses the literal R=20 sums: their
terms include these five central terms and are bounded above by the
infinite sum. Mirroring the coefficient boxes is safe for both; lambda
pair boxes are explicitly hulled before the real Fourier transform.

The integer-only backend uses 256-bit dyadic outward intervals. Basic
arithmetic is implemented by Python integer products, exact Fraction
ratios, floor, and ceiling. Transcendental bounds use:
* exp(-t): reduce t to [0,1/8] by powers of two; bracket it between the
  50-term even Taylor sum and the next odd sum, then square outward.
* pi: Machin's identity 16*atan(1/5)-4*atan(1/239), each with 100 terms of
  the alternating arctangent series and its next-term bound.
* cos: reduce turns modulo one, pair +/- frequencies, and use the
  degree-120 Taylor polynomial with remainder at most
  (22/7)^122/122!. Integral and half-integral turns use exact +/-1.
* log q: write q=2^k*y, 1<=y<2, and z=(y-1)/(y+1). The first N=110 terms
  2 sum_{j=0}^{N-1} z^(2j+1)/(2j+1) have omitted positive tail at most
  2*z^(2N+1)/((2N+1)*(1-z^2)), bounded uniformly using z<=1/3.
All series operations are outward. Every kernel and rho lower endpoint is
positive. The resulting normalization interval includes one. The symbolic
normalization of the infinite law follows from the finite Fourier
identities: convolution by K maps rho^2 to 30*m2*rho, and the mean of rho^3
is m3. No numerical normalization check is substituted for that identity.
No floating transcendental value is used to prove the sign of the true
obstruction. Code: rational_law.py; receipt: rational_law_obstruction.json.
The grid obstruction additionally has a separate exact-Fraction atanh
calculation in exact_obstruction.py.

4. Valid node bounds and the zero-cell correction

At a node let A be forced on, O forced off, W0 free. Set
 F(V)=2 sum_i h(r_i(V))+2 sum_j h(c_j(V)),
 S(W)=F(A union W)-F(A)-gamma(W), W subset W0.
S is normalized and submodular: h is concave and each marginal is a
nonnegative modular mass sum; subtracting a modular price preserves
submodularity. For any permutation of W0, its greedy marginal vector v
satisfies v(W)<=S(W). This follows by adding members of W in permutation
order and applying diminishing returns to each increment. A convex
combination x of such greedy vectors is therefore a valid minorant.
For every completion U=A union W,
 residual(U)>=F(A)-gamma(A)+x(W)+(m(A)+m(W))*log(m(A)+m(W))-phi(m(A)+m(W)).
Relax the free indicators to [0,1] at fixed free mass. Sorting x_z/p_z
minimizes the linear term by fractional knapsack. On each knapsack
segment/window intersection, use the supporting inequality
 t*log t >= (1+log q)*t-q, q>0,
then minimize the resulting affine function on its closed interval.
The chosen q may be any positive proposal; no accuracy of float exp/log is
needed. Proof code recomputes log q outward and all final algebra exactly.

If a free cell z has exact grid mass zero and gamma_z<=0, remove it from
any completion. Its mass and cost do not change and reinserting the cell increases the residual by -gamma_z>=0. Thus omitting all such free
cells is a valid reduction. Forced-on zero cells may remain in the base.
The exact d15 grid orbit has this property. Assigning surrogate masses
1e-30 instead makes the weighted minimum-norm metric dominated by this
modular orbit: about 2.77e20, versus ordinary node norms about 11--21.
The ablation observes immediate numerical termination with one greedy
vertex and very weak bounds. Removing the zeros restores useful proposals.
This repairs numerical conditioning; it does not change the ideal
mathematical optimum of the original base-polytope relaxation.

The second candidate retains integrality in an equal-mass free group H.
If its mass per cell is a, and d_k is the sum of its k smallest x values,
then minimize, over integer k and the remaining fractional mass mu,
 F(A)-gamma(A)+d_k+sigma_rest(mu)+psi(m(A)+k*a+mu)-phi(m(A)+k*a+mu).
Every actual completion is included, so this is valid. The relaxation is
contained in the original fractional one and is at least as strong in
exact arithmetic. The sampled d0 gains are negligible; code orbit.py and
integer_node_engine.py, comparison orbit_comparison.json.

5. Fresh true-law transfer for the successful families

Let Delta bound the L1 difference between the exact grid and rebuilt true
law. It is computed by summing exact per-cell maximum endpoint errors,
then rounded upward to a compact dyadic. Here Delta<1/4, so h is increasing throughout the error range. For any fixed block, the sums of
row errors and column errors are each at most Delta. For 0<=x,y<=1 and
|x-y|<=1/2,
 |h(x)-h(y)|<=h(|x-y|).
This follows from concavity: the largest positive increment is h(delta),
and the largest negative increment is h(1-delta)<=h(delta) for delta<=1/2.
Jensen's inequality therefore gives the block-cost error allowance
 E_block=4*n*h(Delta/n)+h(Delta).
A true mass in window (lo,hi] has grid mass in [lo-Delta,hi+Delta]. The
checker tests every such expanded closed window and requires its grid
residual lower bound to exceed E_block. This proves the actual true-law
block inequality, including blocks whose mass crosses a price edge.
The free-zero reduction is applied only on the grid; the transfer is
charged for the whole original block, so positive true d15 masses are
not discarded.

6. Coverage, receipts, and controls

For an L family, each cyclic line interval of length L+1 forbids all ones.
For an L1_G family, each cyclic interval of length G+1 forbids all zeros.
A separate unit-clause propagator reconstructs all forced decisions from
each original root and recorded branch path. Branch variables agree at
shared prefixes. No leaf is an ancestor of another, both branches exist
at every internal prefix, and the exact Kraft sum is one. These facts
prove coverage of each checked root family. Stored leaf bounds and
acceptance flags receive no proof credit. Every greedy order is a checked
permutation; simplex weights are exact nonnegative integers summing to a
checked denominator. Receipts bind prices, law, code, cover and proposals.

Tests evaluate every nonempty n4 block on five random positive even
circulant laws and 2000 associated random nodes; a separate outward test
covers all 65535 n4 blocks under 16 disjoint first-cell roots. n12 tests
use 100000 random completions under 1000 numerical nodes and an additional
100000 outward-checked completions under 100 nodes. Adding one nat to the
new integral bound is deliberately invalid and is caught. Additional
controls reject a stale price identity, a forged stored bound with changed
prices, malformed windows, missing leaves, inconsistent branching,
invalid greedy permutations and invalid simplex weights. Synthetic exact
mass and shifted-mass controls exercise the larger-adjacent penalty and
Delta-expanded windows. Integer-only exp/cos/log bounds pass 3000
independent high-precision controls; exact integral-turn cosine identities
are checked against their exact values, since an independent pi interval
produces a wider enclosure of those same values.

7. Campaign consequence

A full successful cover at the unchanged point does not exist. Node counts
cannot be projected into a successful certificate at that point. This
does not refute C>2 for the law. Capping all 306 positive penalties at zero
leaves the exact mass-packing minimum unchanged, but the d0_L10 and d0_L15
counterexamples show that this repair alone is insufficient. Adding 16
new witness columns to the stored pool leaves its numerical LP value near
2.006248. The campaign should add the witnesses, re-freeze a price, and use
the corrected zero-cell proposal engine with fresh, bound input receipts.
The pool result is numerical evidence, not complete dual feasibility.



8. Zero omission and logical family constraints

For every certified sample family, the distinguished line has positive grid
mass. Its run/gap clauses therefore contain no omitted grid-zero cell.
The numerical omission cannot alter those logical propagations. This is
checked explicitly in zero_semantics_controls.json. A future all-root
extension must keep the logical on/off state separate from bound-only zero
omission. The n6 alternating-line control in safe_zero_propagation.py shows
that feeding artificial zero-cell off assignments into gap propagation can
falsely make a nonempty family vacuous. The safe_node helper propagates the
original logical assignments first and constructs a separate bound_off mask.
The dominance inequality itself is unchanged. Every n4 block of a law with
a genuine zero-mass diagonal also passes the exact reduction check.

replacement_proposal.json is a different price. It is constructed from the
augmented numerical pool, with an exact dyadic per-cell reserve, and its
conditional scalar is about 2.0052480781. The 14 recorded counterexample
checks pass, and full true-law sample checks pass on d0_L10 (1145 leaves)
and d0_L15 (664 leaves); see replacement_family_certificates_v2.json.
A weak independently generated proposal at one d0_L10 leaf was rejected.

valid simplex minorant, which was then recomputed by the integer checker.
No stored numerical bound was used for acceptance. Other sampled families
remain unfinished after their search caps. This is not an all-block
certificate and does not change the obstruction at the original price.
